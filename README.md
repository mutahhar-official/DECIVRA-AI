# 🤖 Decivra AI

### Multi-Agent Business Intelligence & Decision Support System

**Decivra AI** is an AI-powered multi-agent workflow designed to transform business data into meaningful insights, identify potential risks, generate strategic recommendations, and support better business decisions.

Decivra uses specialized AI agents that work together in a structured procedure

---

### 📊 Business Analyst Agent
Analyzes the provided data to identify trends, problems, and opportunities.


### ⚠️ Risk Analyst Agent
Examines the business analysis to identify potential financial, sales, customer, product, and operational risks.


### 🎯 Strategy Agent
Converts business insights and risks into practical strategies and recommendations.


### 🧠 Decision Agent
Produce a final decision-oriented response.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **Multi-Agent AI Architecture** — Multiple specialized agents collaborate through a structured workflow.
- 🧠 **AI-Assisted Decision Support** — Converts multiple AI perspectives into structured decisions.
- 📋 **Action Plan Generation** — Provides practical strategies and next steps based on identified problems and strategies.
- 📁 **File Upload Support** — Allows users to provide business information through uploaded files for analysis.
- 💬 **Natural-Language Interaction** — Users can ask business questions without requiring technical knowledge.
- 🔍 **Evidence-Based Analysis** — The workflow focuses on provided information and avoids intentionally inventing business data.

## 🎯 Project Goals

1. **Transform business data into actionable insights** through AI-powered analysis.
2. **Help identify risks and opportunities** before they become larger business problems.
3. **Support better decision-making** by combining analysis, risk assessment, strategy, and action planning in one workflow.

---

## 🌟 Why Choose Decivra?

Unlike a traditional AI chatbot that simply generates a response, Decivra divides the business problem into specialized stages.

**Instead of:**

`Question → AI → Answer`

**Decivra uses:**

`Business Data → Analysis → Risk → Strategy → Decision → Action Plan`

This multi-agent approach provides a more structured way to analyze business situations and turn information into practical decisions.
